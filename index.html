About ArtiSpark

Artispark was founded by a creative duo – Goral Shah and Prachi Shah – brought together by their shared passion for art and creativity.

Goral is a digital artist who beautifully blends traditional art with modern digital techniques, bringing innovation and fresh perspectives into the creative space. Her approach focuses on making art relevant in today’s digital world while keeping its artistic essence alive.

Prachi, a trained civil engineer by profession, brings precision, structure, and deep artistic skill to Artispark. With her strong foundation in drawing and painting, she helps students build solid fundamentals while nurturing their creativity and confidence.

Together, Goral and Prachi have created a vibrant community where artists of all ages can explore their creativity, learn new skills, and connect with others who share their passion for art. Their combined expertise ensures that every class and workshop at Artispark is thoughtfully designed to inspire and empower artists on their creative journeys.

Our home studio is a welcoming space where creativity flourishes, connections are made, and artistic skills are crafted with care and passion. Whether you're a child taking your first steps into the world of art, an adult exploring a new hobby, or a group looking for a unique experience, we're here to guide and inspire you.

OUR SERVICES

TAKE A DEEP BREATH

Your creative journey begins here. We've established a space where your artistic potential can flourish, guided by our experienced instructors and a supportive community.

CHILDREN'S ART CLASSES

We run engaging kids’ art classes from our home studios in Wantirna and Springvale. Our sessions provide a warm, encouraging environment where young artists can explore their creativity while learning essential art skills.
Our classes are thoughtfully designed to build confidence, encourage self-expression, and develop creativity through drawing, painting, and fun art techniques. We focus not just on creating artwork, but on helping children enjoy the process, think creatively, and feel proud of what they create.

PAINT 'N SIP

Our Paint & Sip sessions are relaxed, guided art experiences perfect for groups, parties, and special occasions. No prior art experience is needed – just bring your enthusiasm, unwind, and enjoy the creative process.
We provide step-by-step guidance in a fun and supportive atmosphere, making it easy for everyone to create their own masterpiece while socialising and having a great time.

MINDFUL MANDALAS

Our Mindful Mandalas sessions offer a calming and enriching artistic journey where you learn beautiful patterns while fully immersing yourself in the creative process. This structured set of 5 sessions begins with basic mandala patterns and gradually guides you toward creating your own detailed mandala canvas painting.
These sessions are designed to promote focus, patience, and mindfulness while helping you build confidence in pattern creation and design techniques. Whether you are a beginner or an art enthusiast, this workshop provides a relaxing space to learn and create at your own pace.

ADULT WORKSHOPS

Explore various artistic techniques from painting to mixed media. Discover your creative passion in a supportive studio environment.

PAINT PARTIES

Make your events truly memorable with our guided paint sessions, designed to bring creativity, fun, and connection to your special occasions. After all, who doesn’t love creating something they can proudly call their own?
We bring the complete art experience to you – all materials and supplies are provided, so you can simply relax, enjoy the moment, and create beautiful memories with us.
